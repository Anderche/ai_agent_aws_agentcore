Corporate Filing AI Agent

Explore Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filings from the EDGAR (Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval) database & Chat with the LangGraph-powered assistant.

What this app does

This assistant helps operations and compliance teams explore SEC filings. Search EDGAR by ticker for forms such as 10-K and 10-Q, then ask questions about a filing once it is split into searchable chunks. It also answers from a saved FAQ, remembers the conversation, and can file a support ticket, post to Slack, or open a review request.
