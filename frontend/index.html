C

Corporate Filing AI Agent

Explore Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filings from the EDGAR (Electronic Data Gathering, Analysis, and Retrieval) database & Chat with the LangGraph-powered assistant.

What this app does

  • Finds a company's SEC record (CIK) from its stock ticker.
  • Lists recent EDGAR filings, filterable by form type or year.
  • Prepares a chosen filing so you can ask questions about it.
  • Answers common questions from a saved FAQ.
  • Remembers earlier messages within the current session.
  • Files support tickets, Slack alerts, or review requests when those are configured.

What this app does not do

  • Give financial, legal, or investment advice.
  • Show live stock prices or market data.
  • Cover companies or documents outside the SEC's EDGAR database.
  • Recognize tickers shorter than 3 or longer than 4 letters.
  • Keep conversations after the server restarts.
  • Run without limits; this demo caps replies, prepared filings, and filing chats.
  • Guarantee accuracy; answers can miss or misread details in a filing.

Demo purposes only. Not to be misconstrued as financial advice. Designed by Anders Kiss. Codebase is here.
